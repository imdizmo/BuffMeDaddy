# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- Buff profiles: save the effects you have on (each with its no-time-limit switch) under a name; one click gives them all again, to you or the player you picked.
- The tab in the Jatify design, built from AdminHammer 1.1's panel parts; the target is the panel's Target picker.
- No longer needs SeneaL UI. Needs AdminHammer 1.1.0.

## 1.0.1

- New icon: a mead flask. No code changes.

## 1.0.0

- First release: a Buffs tab for AdminHammer. Give or remove any status effect, with a per-effect no-time-limit switch.
- Give buffs to other players (works without mods on their side); no time limit for them keeps the effect topped up.
